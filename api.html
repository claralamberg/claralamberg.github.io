The concept

Where is fashion
happening — and
can I afford to go?

Fashion Flight Finder combines two different datasets to create a new way of discovering fashion destinations.

Modemonline provides the context: what is happening, where and when. Flygresor.se adds the practical layer: how much it costs to get there.

Together, the two sources create a service that connects cultural discovery with travel planning.
